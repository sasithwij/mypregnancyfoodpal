About Food Pal

Food Pal tells you if a food is OK to eat while pregnant, and why.

Avoid means don't eat it. Caution means it's fine only in the way described, so read the box. OK means go ahead.

It's based only on the NSW Food Authority pregnancy brochure (2023) and the Royal Women's Hospital fact sheet (March 2026). Where they differ, the stricter advice wins.

Photo checks are done by AI against the same rules. AI can misread a photo, so if anything looks wrong, ask the staff.

General information only, not medical advice. For advice specific to you, talk to your doctor, midwife or obstetrician.
